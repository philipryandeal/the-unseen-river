# Last page of Stretch 01

*Handed downstream by David Bear, November 11, 2026. This is the final page of the stretch above yours, word for word. It is all you get.*

---

Below the last cedar the ground stops falling so fast, and the water notices. It has been a trickle between stones all morning. Here it becomes a brook: two hand-widths across, ankle deep, quick enough to carry things and too young to have a name.

It is cold, clear all the way to the bottom, and it runs over grey gravel with a sound like someone turning pages.

Three things are floating in it when it leaves my hands.

A cedar needle, going down sideways.

A grey feather from a bird I never saw.

And a boat folded out of one sheet of paper, small enough to sit in a palm. There is writing on the inside of the fold. I did not open it, and I am not going to tell you what I think it says.

The brook goes around a bend to the left, into ground I can't see from here. It is yours now.

— David Bear
