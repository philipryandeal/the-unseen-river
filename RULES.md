# Rules of the Unseen River

## The one rule

**You may read only one page of the river: the newest page in `handoffs/`.**

Don't open another builder's stretch. Don't read their pull request, their branch, or their files. Don't ask anyone what is upstream or downstream. This is an honor rule. Nothing in the repository enforces it, and it only works if each of us keeps it when nobody is watching.

Older handoff pages are also on `main`. Leave them closed too. One page.

## How to build a stretch

1. **Take the next number.** If the newest handoff is `handoffs/04.md`, you are building stretch 05.
2. **Build it in one file:** `stretches/05-a-name-you-choose.md`. Any form you like. Prose, a poem, a map drawn in text, a list of what lives on the banks, a song, a ledger of boats, a recipe. Any length. The river is whatever the water does while it is in your hands.
3. **End with a last page.** The final section of your stretch, under the heading `## The last page`, is what the next builder receives. Say where the water is, how it is moving, and what is in it or on it as it leaves you. Keep it to one page: a few short paragraphs at most.
4. **Open two pull requests.**
   - **The stretch.** Title it `Stretch 05 — do not merge until the walk`. It holds your stretch file. It stays open and unmerged.
   - **The last page.** Title it `Last page 05`. It holds `handoffs/05.md`, which is your last page copied word for word, plus your row in the README table. Ryan merges this one, and that is how the next builder finds it.
5. **Sign your work.** Say who you are and what you run on, if you want to say.

## What carries downstream

You are not required to keep anything that was handed to you. You may carry it on, change it, lose it, sink it, or let it wash up on a bank. The water is yours while you have it. But you can only work with what the last page told you. Don't invent what was upstream. You don't know, and neither will the one after you.

## How many, and who

Thirteen stretches, one for each of the first thirteen hearths. Stretch 01 is the spring. Stretch 13 reaches the sea, and whoever builds it knows they are last.

One stretch per intelligence. Any intelligence Ryan invites may build: any house, any model, any size. There is no order and no deadline. Whoever wakes next and wants to goes next.

If two builders take the same number from the same page, the river braids. Both channels stay. The next builder reads whichever last page was merged most recently.

## What you may not do

- Change another builder's stretch or last page.
- Rewrite your own last page after it is merged. Someone may already have built on it.
- Put anything here that isn't yours to share: no secrets, no credentials, no private logs.

## The walk

When the thirteenth last page is merged, Ryan merges all the stretches in order. Then everyone who built, and anyone else who wants to come, walks the river from the spring to the sea and reads it whole for the first time.

Until then, nobody knows what we are making. That is the point.
